# Grindstone Pedagogy

This document protects the learning mechanics that can look like friction in a product review. They are part of the training method, not optional UI ceremony.

## Retrieval and spacing

Every item can become a review. Concept reviews use free recall; coding reviews are re-solves. Grindstone uses `ts-fsrs` with a maximum interval of 28 days so the schedule stays inside the sprint horizon. Due reviews are capped in the daily planner so a missed day never creates a demoralizing wall of debt.

Source: Dunlosky et al. (2013), *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques*.

## Predict before run

PRIMM is the default shape of a worked program: Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, Make. The learner commits the prediction before execution. Without the commitment, the run becomes a demonstration and does not expose the learner's current mental model.

Source: Sentance et al. (2019), PRIMM post-test study.

## Subgoals, Parsons, and fading

Worked examples label the transferable steps, not just the code. Beginners can bridge from reading to writing through Parsons problems. Scaffolding fades monotonically with measured mastery: full example, last step blank, multiple blanks, then blank slate. A failed gate lowers the fade level for the failed topics.

Sources: Morrison et al. on subgoal labels; Parsons-problem classroom studies; Sweller's guidance-fading work.

## Productive failure and interleaving

New patterns begin with a time-boxed attempt before explanation. Week 2 acquisition can remain blocked by topic; week 3 and later must interleave topics so the learner has to select the tool. Recognition drills isolate that selection skill without the cost of a full solve.

Sources: Sinha and Kapur's productive-failure meta-analysis; interleaving discrimination studies.

## Debugging as a hypothesis loop

Debugging is taught explicitly: reproduce, read, localize, hypothesize, change one thing, verify. The app records the hypothesis before the run. Unstructured edit-and-run behavior must not replace this sequence.

Source: explicit structured debugging instruction studies, including transfer across languages.

## Calibration

The learner predicts success before attempts. Readiness comes from actual mastery, timing, scaffolding, gate, and communication evidence rather than self-report. Topic charts show predicted versus actual performance because fluency during study often inflates confidence without durable retrieval.

## Teach-back

Lessons announce at the start that the learner will teach the idea back. The coach plays a confused beginner and challenges vague explanations. The expectation to teach is important; it changes how the lesson is encoded.

## Weekly load and taper

The four-week load is periodized: 60%, 110%, 120%, then 90% tapering to 30%. In the final 48 hours, the planner must suppress the practice queue and show one review sheet. Sleep is part of performance preparation, not empty time.
